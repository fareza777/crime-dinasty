## Full description

Vice Dynasty is a neo-noir crime life simulator about a family name that outlives any one boss.

1998. You are eighteen in Ravenport, a rain-soaked American port that keeps a ledger for every surname that lasts. The first years are not trivia. They are a campaign: an unclaimed crate on Pier 7, a detective who files in pencil, a loyalty test on a ferry that does not print tickets. Silas Crowe already knows the rain. Detective Rhea Vale does not know your face — yet. Cass Caldera is already measuring the chair.

Year by year the city writes you. Named story chains stretch across a decade — The Quiet Pier, the Crow Ledger, a Glass Marriage, a Red Audit, a Sibling Split, the Empty Chair, the Docks Fire, the Crown Prosecutor. Choices are short and expensive. Heat draws police. Rival houses (the Calderas, the Rooke Outfit, House Vex, the Marrow Kin) grow whether you watch them or not. Children grow traits. Crews keep cuts. Fronts launder a story as much as they launder a year.

When your character dies, retires, or takes a life sentence, the chair does not vanish. You pick an heir in the Hall of Legacy. Wealth, businesses, territory, reputation, enemies, and unfinished weather carry into the next generation.

This is fiction. It is a story engine about power, family, and rain — not a manual for real-world crime.

Features
• A prologue campaign in Ravenport rain, then a life that still has chapters
• Named multi-year story chains with payoffs years later
• Recurring people who remember what you did
• Choice cards with voice, consequences, and district-tagged events
• Crew loyalty, betrayal, and jobs that can fail
• Police heat, court, lawyers, and prison as a playable chapter
• Rival families with crests, moods, turf, and scripted war seasons (threat → escalation → resolution)
• Marriage, children, family conflict, and a drawn genealogy tree
• Fronts from story years and a cash shop on Empire
• Inheritance of the empire — and its enemies
• Hall of Legacy, achievements, and ceremonial endings
• Autosave plus three manual slots
• Fully offline. No account. No always-on server.

Vice Dynasty contains stylized crime, violence, and prison themes appropriate for a mature story game. It does not teach real techniques.

## Content rating notes (IARC / Play questionnaire)

- **Category:** Games — Simulation / Role Playing / Strategy
- **Crime fiction:** The game depicts fictional organized crime, theft, smuggling, robbery, and police response as narrative events. It does not provide real-world instructions, tools, or how-to guidance.
- **Violence:** Stylized. Injuries, rare deaths, intimidation. No gore focus, no sexual violence.
- **Language:** American English, adult but not wall-to-wall profanity.
- **Drugs:** Implied vice / night-world atmosphere; no instructional use, no brand-name controlled substance tutorials. Prefer “night world / clubs” framing. Keep questionnaire honest if any alcohol/club scenes remain.
- **Gambling:** Flavor (card rooms) without real-money gambling or simulated casino economies for cash-out.
- **User interaction:** Offline, no UGC, no chat, no ads targeted at children.
- **Ads:** AdMob rewarded + rare interstitials. No banners. Designed for 17+ audiences. Ads init after first frame and fail soft; AdMob does not start at process launch.
- **Suggested rating:** Mature 17+ (crime, violence, mature themes). Do not target children. Complete the IARC questionnaire; do not self-declare below the crime/violence answers.
